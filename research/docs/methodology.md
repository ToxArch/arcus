# Research Methodology & Evidence Boundary

Snapshot date: **2026-11-11**. Purpose: establish the verified pre-project factual intelligence, mathematical proofs, and statutory standards required prior to system implementation.

## Evidence Scope and Verification Standards

This research corpus strictly documents:
1. **Primary Standards:** Authoritative specifications published by the Internet Engineering Task Force (IETF), Broadband Forum (TR series), and International Telecommunication Union (ITU-T).
2. **Statutory Regimes:** Official Gazette of India notifications, legislative enactments by the Parliament of India (IT Act, 2000; Jan Vishwas Act, 2023; Bharatiya Sakshya Adhiniyam, 2023), and formal directives issued by the Ministry of Electronics and Information Technology (MeitY) and CERT-In.
3. **Malware Disassembly:** Binary reverse-engineering and source code audits from leaked repositories (Mirai leaked September 2016) and threat research analyses published by Qihoo 360 Netlab, Check Point, US-CERT, and academic proceedings (USENIX Security, ACM CCS, IEEE S&P).
4. **Empirical Network Data:** Observable telemetry profiles, line-rate capture physics, and mathematical derivations of log volumes and Bayesian probability distributions.

## Evidence Tiers

| Evidence Tier | Source Standard | Verification Rule |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Tier 1: Sovereign Law & Standards** | Indian Parliamentary Acts, Gazette of India, IETF RFCs | Unconditional statutory or standards compliance requirement. |
| **Tier 2: Primary Code & Disassembly** | Leaked/analyzed source code, binary decompilations | Direct verification of function logic, hardcoded strings, protocols. |
| **Tier 3: Academic & Peer-Reviewed** | USENIX, IEEE, ACM conference proceedings | Verified methodologies, peer-reviewed mathematical formulations. |
| **Tier 4: Vendor & Telemetry Reports** | Cisco, MikroTik, Spamhaus, Team Cymru, abuse.ch | Industry-standard operational telemetry and threat intelligence feeds. |

All sections avoid speculative implementation claims or post-project evaluations.
