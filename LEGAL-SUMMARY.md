### What Kind of License Is This?
ARCUS is distributed under a **source-available, permission-required license**
(SPDX: `LicenseRef-ARCUS-1.0`).

> **Important Distinction:** This is **NOT an open-source license** (it is not
> approved by the Open Source Initiative - OSI) and it is **NOT free software**
> (under FSF guidelines). You have the freedom to view and study the work, but
> you do not have unrestricted freedom to deploy, commercialize, or modify it.

### The Trade-off of This Approach
By choosing a permission-required, source-available license rather than a
standard open-source license (such as Apache 2.0 or MIT), the project gains
complete protection against commercial exploitation and prevents corporate
competitors from taking the research for free. However, the direct trade-off is
that **this project will attract fewer third-party community contributors and
experience slower adoption** than projects distributed under standard permissive
licenses. This is an intentional strategic choice to protect the creator's
intellectual property while the project is in active development.
