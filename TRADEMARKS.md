# ARCUS Trademark and Brand Guidelines

This document sets forth the guidelines and rules governing the use of the
trademarks, names, and logos associated with the ARCUS project.

---

### Reserved Trademarks
The following marks are proprietary trademarks belonging to Dhanush B S / ToxArch:
* **"ARCUS"**
* **"ToxArch"**
* The ARCUS visual identity logo and geometric vector emblems.

---

### Allowed Uses (Without Separate Permission)
You may use the word mark "ARCUS" solely for truthful, factual descriptions of
the project's origin:
* Example: *"This analysis examines the ARCUS Carrier-Grade NAT attribution architecture."*
* Example: *"Our research compares Suricata performance against ARCUS specifications."*
* Hyperlinking directly to https://github.com/ToxArch/arcus.

---

### Disallowed Uses (Requires Prior Written Permission)
* **No Commercial Product Naming:** You cannot name your software, router
  package, hardware appliance, or security service "ARCUS", "ARCUS-Security",
  "ToxArch-ISP", or any confusingly similar variation.
* **No Implied Affiliation:** You cannot display the ToxArch or ARCUS logo on
  your company website, business cards, marketing brochures, or sales decks in
  any way that implies the creator endorses, certifies, or partners with you.
* **No Modified Logos:** You cannot distort, modify, recolor, or combine the
  ARCUS logo with third-party company branding.
* **No Domain Name Registrations:** You cannot register internet domain names
  incorporating "arcus-defense", "toxarch-security", or similar brand marks.
